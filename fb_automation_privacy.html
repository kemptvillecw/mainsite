Privacy Policy

Kemptville Creative Writers Automation App

Our Commitment to Your Privacy

Kemptville Creative Writers (“we”, “our”, “the group”) operates a small automation tool used only to publish content to our official Facebook Page. This app does not collect, store, process, share, or transmit any personal data from Facebook users.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information from Facebook or from people who interact with our Page. We do not access user profiles, messages, friends lists, email addresses, or any other personal data.

How the App Works

The app publishes scheduled or automated posts to our official Facebook Page, including announcements, community updates, and other information relevant to our members and local area. It does not interact with individual users, and it does not perform any actions on behalf of users. All activity is limited strictly to posting content on our own Page.

Data Sharing

Because we do not collect data, we do not share any data with third parties.

Cookies & Tracking

This app does not use cookies, tracking technologies, analytics tools, or advertising systems.

Your Rights

Since no personal data is collected or stored, there is no personal information to access, modify, or delete.
