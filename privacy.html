Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

We believe every writer benefits from transparency about how we handle your information and the terms of our service.

Privacy Policy

What We Collect

When you sign up for the Kemptville Creative Writers (KCW) newsletter, we only ask for:

  • Your email (so we can send the newsletter)
  • Your first name (optional)
  • Your newsletter preferences (as well as whether you want weekly updates or not)
  • When you subscribed and confirmed your subscription

How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to:

  • Send meeting dates and event updates
  • Deliver weekly newsletters (if you choose to receive them)
  • Notify you about schedule changes or library closures
  • Respond to questions you send us

We never sell your information or share it with other companies.

How Your Data Is Stored

  • Your subscription details are kept in a secure Google Sheet.
  • Emails are delivered through a secure email service.
  • We regularly delete old spam/honeypot data.

How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Active subscribers: kept until you unsubscribe
  • Unsubscribed emails: deleted after 90 days
  • Delivery logs: deleted after 30 days

Your Choices

You can:

  • View or update your preferences
  • Unsubscribe anytime
  • Ask us to delete your information

Just use the links at the bottom of any KCW email.

Services We Use

  • Google Sheets & Google Apps Script (for managing subscriptions)
  • Facebook (for sharing newsletter summaries)

Spam protection — Cloudflare Turnstile

We use Cloudflare Turnstile to protect our newsletter subscription form from spam and automated abuse. Turnstile processes technical information, including your IP address, browser information, connection characteristics, and information identifying our website, to distinguish human visitors from bots. Cloudflare also uses these signals to improve its bot detection capabilities.

For more information about how Cloudflare processes this information, see the Cloudflare Turnstile Privacy Addendum and Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Updates to This Policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it on our website.

Contact

Questions? Email us at privacy@kemptvillecreativewriters.com.
