Our story

Creative writing, in community

The Kemptville Creative Writers (KCW) started as the North Grenville Writers Circle, founded in 2018 by Pamela Welling.

Kemptville Creative Writers is a member-led community where writers build knowledge together through discussion, practice, guest presentations, shared experience, and dedicated writing time.

Our members include hobbyists, aspiring novelists, memoirists, and both traditionally and independently published authors.

KCW is a member-led organization. Our programming is shaped by what members want to learn and the projects they are working on.
