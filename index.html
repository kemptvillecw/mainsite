Thinking about your first meeting?

First-Time Visitors

New to KCW? You don't need to prepare anything special. Come to a meeting, meet other writers, and participate as much or as little as feels comfortable.

Learn more about attending →
  • No advance submission required
  • All genres and experience levels welcome
  • Participation is optional and flexible
  • Notebook or laptop optional
  • Attendance is free
  • Meetings are held in the Tallman Room
  • Check the schedule for changes
  • Arrive a few minutes early if you can
